# Niche-Specific Hook Examples

## Real Hooks Organized by Niche

These are actual hooks from faceless scripts across specific niches. Use the hooks from your niche as models.

---

## CRITICAL: Write Conversationally

**Your hooks should sound like someone telling a story, not a copywriter writing ad copy.**

Before using any hook below, ask: "Would I actually say this out loud to a friend?"

If not, rewrite it to flow more naturally. Choppy sentence fragments ("Millions. Billions. Chaos.") sound written. Flowing narrative sounds spoken.

---

## ANTI-AI SLOP: Quick Reference

**Never use these patterns in ANY niche:**

| AI Slop | Human Alternative |
|---------|-------------------|
| "No X. No Y. No Z." fragments | Use commas, vary rhythm |
| "Most founders think..." | Tell a specific story instead |
| "It's not X, it's Y" | Make direct statement (max 1 per script) |
| "Here's the thing:" | Just say the thing |
| "47% of..." / "73% more..." | Use real, sourced numbers |
| "Powerful" / "Game-changing" | Show specific outcome |
| "Let that sink in" | Delete it |
| "What most people don't realize..." | Speak from experience |
| "I spent the last [time] doing..." | Start with the discovery, not the process |

**The Test:** Would you say this at a coffee shop? If no, rewrite it.

## SPORTS - Basketball/WNBA

### The Rivalry Hook
**Pattern:** Rivalry context + Jealousy angle + "But is it working?"

```
Okay, so, it's the Angel Reese and Caitlyn Clark rivalry once again.
Ever since Clark became one of the most famous WNBA players, Angel
Reese's jealousy has increased significantly, and Angel Reese isn't even
hiding it anymore. She's desperately trying to take credit for bringing
women's basketball on the map and in a recent interview, she indirectly
asked everyone to stop glorifying just one person, Caitlyn Clark.
```

**Why it works:**
- Names both players immediately (searchable)
- "Jealousy" is a strong emotional hook
- Sets up the conflict viewers want to see

### The Legend Endorsement Hook
**Pattern:** Legend says X about player + Why this matters

```
When Shaq O'Neal compliments your game, that's a pretty good sign that
you're doing something right. And Shaq isn't the only basketball legend
to be amazed by Caitlin Clark's performances. Even LeBron James was
shocked to see how well Clark handled herself on the court.
```

## GAMING & ESPORTS

### The Cheating Allegations Hook
**Pattern:** Controversy + Despite allegations + Massive achievement

```
Peterbot has become one of the best players in the world, even though
everyone thinks he's been cheating for years. But despite the
allegations, he managed to win one of the toughest, most challenging
Fortnite tournaments at the young age of 16. His opponents? Fortnite
legends like Clix, Bugha, Kanada, and several others. So, how did he win
this tournament? Did he really use hacks? Or is he just that good?
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with controversy (cheating allegations)
- "Despite" creates underdog narrative
- Names specific legends (credibility + searchability)
- Ends with questions viewers need answered

### The Age Achievement Hook
**Pattern:** Young age + Massive achievement + "How did they do it?"

```
[SUBJECT] was just [AGE] years old when they [ACHIEVEMENT].
Everyone said it was impossible. But [NUMBER] months later, they had
[SPECIFIC RESULT]. This is how a teenager became [TITLE/STATUS].
```

## MOVIES & FILM / HOLLYWOOD

### The "Dark Side of Fame" Listicle Hook
**Pattern:** Admiration setup + Dark truth + List promise

```
Celebrities are admired by everyone. Whether they make us laugh or amaze
us with their incredible talent, we look up to these stars and
occasionally envy their success. However, there is often a dark side to
fame. From actors to other famous people, these stars' careers were high
all the time.
```

**Why it works:**
- Establishes positive image first
- "However" creates the turn
- "Dark side" promises revelation

### The Redemption Arc Hook
**Pattern:** Criminal past + Impossible comeback + Current success

```
Few transitions from Hollywood bad boy to international role model have
been as spectacular as Robert Downey Jr.'s. In less than ten years, he
went from serving time in state prison to being one of the most
well-known superheroes in film history.
```

## WAR & CONFLICT / HISTORY

### The Historical Tragedy Hook
**Pattern:** Date + Scale of tragedy + "This is the story of..."

```
On September 11, 2001, fear surrounded the USA when four hijacked planes
attacked the World Trade Center Twin Towers and the Pentagon. The Twin
Towers were two of the tallest buildings in the world at the time.
Collectively, more than 2,900 people were killed that day, making it the
deadliest and most horrifying terrorist attack in the history of the US.
This is the story of the World Trade Center attacks, famously known as
the 9/11 attacks.
```

**Why it works:**
- Specific date anchors the event
- Scale of tragedy (2,900 killed, deadliest ever)
- "This is the story of..." frames the narrative

### The Mastermind Hook
**Pattern:** Who was behind it + Their reasoning + The plan

```
It was not [OBVIOUS PERSON] who originally came up with the attack. In
fact, it was [ACTUAL MASTERMIND], a [NATIONALITY] who later became known
as [THEIR ROLE]. The plan was presented in [YEAR], however, it was not
until [LATER YEAR] that the attacks were approved.
```

## TRUE CRIME & SERIAL KILLERS

### The Conversational Mystery Hook (RECOMMENDED)
**Pattern:** Tell the story naturally + Stack the mystery + End on what we don't know

```
In 1971, a man walked onto a plane, handed a flight attendant a note, and
told her there was a bomb in his briefcase.

Four hours later, he jumped out the back with $200,000 in cash... into a
freezing thunderstorm... and was never seen again.

The FBI spent 45 years hunting him. They interviewed thousands of suspects,
tested DNA, chased every lead. And to this day, nobody knows who he was,
whether he survived, or where the money went.
```

**Why it works:**
- Flows like someone telling you a story
- Stakes build naturally through the narrative
- Ends on triple mystery (identity, survival, money)

### The Legendary Escape Hook (Listicle Format)
**Pattern:** Expected outcome + "But what about..." + Legends who escaped

```
We all have seen movies and cases of cops and legendary policemen who go
out of their way to catch criminals. But what about the people who have
outwitted the police itself? Who have dodged the justice system as we
know it? These are some of the legends that outwitted the police and
actually got away with it.
```

**When to use:** Setting up a listicle of multiple escape stories

## CELEBRITY SCANDALS & DRAMA

### The Conversational Biographical Hook (RECOMMENDED)
**Pattern:** Peak dominance → Fall from grace → "However" turn to present day

```
At 20 years old, Mike Tyson became the youngest heavyweight champion in
history. His opponents weren't just losing. They were being carried out
on stretchers.

By the time he was 25, he'd made $300 million and scared an entire
generation of fighters into retirement.

But then came the rape conviction, the bankruptcy, and the night he bit
off Evander Holyfield's ear on pay-per-view. Everyone wrote him off.

So why, at 58 years old, did 60 million people just pay to watch him
fight again?
```

**Why it works:**
- Reads like a documentary narrator
- Ends on a question that pulls viewers in
- Specific numbers add weight

### The Emotional Investment Hook (RECOMMENDED FOR VIRAL SENSATIONS)
**Pattern:** Make them feel the high → Then reveal the fall

For viral celebrities and internet personalities, build emotional investment BEFORE the betrayal.

```
For three weeks in June, she was everywhere. The clip got 50 million views.
She was on every podcast, every morning show. Brands were fighting over her.
Everyone wanted to be the one who "discovered" her next.

That was six months ago.

Today, she's mass-unfollowed, dropped by every sponsor, and the same
platforms that made her famous are pretending she never existed. What
happened? And who actually made money when she went viral?
```

**Why this works better than listing controversies:**
- Builds emotional investment first ("everywhere," "everyone wanted")
- Time skip creates curiosity ("That was six months ago")
- Questions at end demand answers
- "Who actually made money" hints at deeper story

### The In Media Res Documentary Hook
**Pattern:** Controversy list + Career context + "However, fame comes with..."

```
Cheating allegations, inappropriate content, and using minors to promote
her content. McKinley Richardson has made quite a career out of all of
these things. She started dating YouTuber and Kick streamer Jack Doherty
not so long ago, catapulting her to celebrity status. Today, she has
millions of followers on her socials. However, with fame comes
controversy, and Richardson is surrounded by it right now.
```

**Note:** The In Media Res hook works for established celebrities with known controversies. For viral sensations or internet personalities, use the Emotional Investment Hook instead.

### The Rags to Riches Hook
**Pattern:** Simple activity + Billionaire result + Scale

```
Toys, games, snacks, and trips to Legoland. These activities turned a
3-year-old into a YouTube superstar and a billionaire in a matter of few
years. Ryan Kaji, who is by far the most beloved kids YouTuber today,
has amassed a wealth of 5 billion dollars by doing random activities.
At the age of 12, he has one of the biggest YouTube channels on the
platform.
```

## BUSINESS & MONEY

### The Conversational Collapse Hook (RECOMMENDED)
**Pattern:** Build trust through credibility → Flip it → Rapid-fire collapse

```
Tom Brady told you to trust them. So did Steph Curry, Larry David, and
Shaq. FTX spent hundreds of millions convincing the world they were the
future of finance.

And for a while, everyone believed it. The company was worth $32 billion.
Sam Bankman-Fried was on the cover of Forbes.

Then one tweet changed everything. Within 72 hours, FTX was bankrupt,
customer funds had vanished, and Sam was in handcuffs.
```

**Why it works:**
- Celebrity names build credibility, then betray it
- "Everyone believed it" makes viewer feel included
- Final line lands three punches in one sentence

### The Proof-First Hook with Mini-Payoff Preview
**Pattern:** Specific result + Mini-payoff preview + Promise of how + Credibility

The key improvement: Give them ONE insight immediately, then promise more.

**Basic version:**
```
My eCommerce brand made a record 2.5 million dollars in revenue in the
last 30 days. And today, I'm gonna share the ten eCom apps that played a
crucial role helping me make that happen.
```

**Better version with mini-payoff:**
```
MrBeast makes over $700 million a year. And most of it doesn't come from
YouTube ad revenue. His real business model is something most creators
completely ignore, and it's the same strategy that turned a guy filming
himself giving away money into one of the wealthiest entertainers alive.

One example: Feastables. He launched a chocolate bar company that did
$10 million in its first few months. Not because he advertised it. Because
he built it INTO his content.

That's the playbook. Here's how it actually works.
```

**Why the improved version works:**
- Mini-payoff delivered immediately (Feastables example)
- Viewer gets value before the video even starts
- Creates trust that the rest will deliver too
- "That's the playbook" creates promise of systematic breakdown

### The Relevance Bridge Hook
**Pattern:** Why this matters to YOU specifically + Then the story

For business content, always bridge to viewer relevance before diving into the subject.

```
If you've ever wondered why some creators blow up while others with better
content stay invisible, this story explains everything.

MrBeast didn't become the biggest YouTuber by accident. He spent years
studying the algorithm, testing thumbnails, and building a system that
most creators will never understand. And the craziest part? He's been
sharing the exact playbook in interviews for years.
```

**Why this works:**
- Opens with viewer's problem ("why do some blow up while others don't")
- Creates relevance before introducing subject
- "He's been sharing for years" implies accessible knowledge

### The Hidden Truth Hook
**Pattern:** What you see + What's really happening + Investigation promise

```
Dubai is one of the most wealthy cities in the world, renowned for being
the most visited city in the United Arab Emirates. However, many people
are unaware of Dubai's darker side, which lies behind all the glitz and
money. What dark secrets does Dubai hide? Let's investigate.
```

## HIP-HOP & RAP

### The Beef/Rivalry Hook
**Pattern:** Rivalry established + Origin + Escalation

```
50 Cent and P. Diddy's rivalry has escalated in recent times, with 50
Cent attracting Diddy's attention after he wanted to join Bad Boy
Records, but Diddy declined after a meeting. Not being signed with
Diddy, 50 Cent then went on to be signed with Eminem and had a really
successful career.

### The "Times When" Listicle Hook
**Pattern:** Most extreme example + Range of incidents + Promise

```
From pulling a gun on the interviewer, to squaring up, rappers nowadays
are willing to do anything they can to check on people who are trying to
ask them too many personal questions. These are 10 rappers who
disrespected interviewers.

## BIZARRE & CULTS

### The Dark Humor Hook
**Pattern:** Name the cult + Obvious absurdity + Ironic death

```
Our first cult is the Appalachian Snake Church, and you're never gonna
guess what reptile they're obsessed with. Snakes. It's snakes. This
church started in the early 1900s, founded by George Went Hensly who
died of old age in 1955... just kidding. He died to a rattlesnake bite.
Who would've guessed, right? Absolute shocker.
```

### The Belief Setup Hook
**Pattern:** Strange belief stated plainly + Scale of commitment

```
Forget about Instagram fame or moving up in your job, these people chose
to focus on what they really wanted in life: catching a ride on a
spaceship supposedly hiding behind the Hale-Bopp comet. Marshall
Applewhite saw himself as a messenger with "divine" insight, responsible
for guiding his followers to the "Next Level."
```

## F1 & MOTORSPORTS

### The Scandal Tease Hook
**Pattern:** What the sport is known for + "But the scandals!" + Promise

```
Formula One racing is a supercharged thrill trip that keeps us on the
tip of our seats with each twist and turn on the circuit. But what
actually raises the enthusiasm level? The scandals! They infuse an
exhilarating dose of adrenaline, propelling spectators into a state of
anticipation. And in today's episode, we are going to dive right into
the juiciest scandals of Formula One Racing.
```

### The "Name the Villain" Hook (RECOMMENDED)
**Pattern:** Name the perpetrator + Specific victims + Scale of betrayal + Ongoing consequences

For sports scandals, don't just describe "controversy." Name names, identify victims, and show what's still at stake.

```
In 2006, Luciano Moggi thought he was untouchable. As the general manager
of Juventus, he had referees on speed dial, literally picking which ones
would officiate his team's matches.

By the time investigators finished, they'd uncovered 100,000 phone calls.
Juventus was stripped of two Serie A titles. Four other clubs faced
relegation. And careers that took decades to build were destroyed
overnight.

The fallout is still shaping Italian football today. This is Calciopoli.
```

**Why this works better than vague scandal hooks:**
- Names the villain immediately (Luciano Moggi)
- Shows specific evidence (100,000 phone calls)
- Lists concrete victims (Juventus stripped, 4 clubs relegated)
- "Still shaping" creates ongoing relevance
- Name of scandal at end creates recognition payoff

## UNIVERSAL ELEMENTS

Every niche hook should contain:

1. **Specific names** (searchable, credible)
2. **Specific numbers** ($2.5M, 2,900 killed, age 16)
3. **Tension word** (however, but, despite, although)
4. **Curiosity gap** (question or promise of revelation)
