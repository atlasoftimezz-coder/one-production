# Celebrity Documentary Faceless Scripts

> **OUTPUT MODE NOTE:** Visual cues like `[CLIP:]` and `[B-ROLL:]` shown here are for **Traditional mode**. For **Vidrush mode**, use the same structures but embed visual keywords naturally in prose instead. See master file for details.

## The Celebrity Documentary Formula

Celebrity content thrives on the gap between public image and private reality. Your job is to bridge that gap while keeping viewers hooked.

---

## CRITICAL: Write Conversationally

**Your hooks should sound like someone telling a story, not a copywriter writing ad copy.**

Before using any hook, ask: "Would I actually say this out loud to a friend?"

If not, rewrite it to flow more naturally. Choppy sentence fragments ("Scandal. Millions. Downfall.") sound written. Flowing narrative sounds spoken.

---

## HOOK TYPES FOR CELEBRITY DOCS

### Type 1: The Conversational Biographical Hook (RECOMMENDED)

**Pattern:** Peak dominance → Fall from grace → "However" turn to present day

```
At 20 years old, Mike Tyson became the youngest heavyweight champion in
history. His opponents weren't just losing. They were being carried out
on stretchers.

By the time he was 25, he'd made $300 million and scared an entire
generation of fighters into retirement.

But then came the rape conviction, the bankruptcy, and the night he bit
off Evander Holyfield's ear on pay-per-view. Everyone wrote him off.

So why, at 58 years old, did 60 million people just pay to watch him
fight again?
```

**Why it works:**
- Reads like a documentary narrator, not a copywriter
- "However" turn built naturally into the narrative
- Ends on a question that pulls viewers in (curiosity gap)
- Specific numbers (20 years old, $300M, 60 million) add weight

**When to use:** Biographical documentaries, comeback stories, celebrity deep dives

---

### Type 2: The In Media Res Hook

**Pattern:** Rapid controversy list + Fame context + "However" turn

```
Cheating allegations, inappropriate content, and using minors to promote
her content. McKinley Richardson has made quite a career out of all of
these things. She started dating YouTuber and Kick streamer Jack Doherty
not so long ago, catapulting her to celebrity status. Today, she has
millions of followers on her socials. However, with fame comes
controversy, and Richardson is surrounded by it right now.
```

**Template:**
```
[Controversy 1], [controversy 2], and [controversy 3].
[CELEBRITY NAME] has [built a career / become famous] from all of these.
[Brief context of their rise].
Today, [current status - followers, fame, net worth].
However, [tension point that opens the story].
```

**Word count:** 75-100 words max

---

### Type 3: The Rags-to-Riches Hook

**Pattern:** Simple origin + Massive result + Scale

```
Toys, games, snacks, and trips to Legoland. These activities turned a
3-year-old into a YouTube superstar and a billionaire in a matter of few
years. Ryan Kaji, who is by far the most beloved kids YouTuber today,
has amassed a wealth of 5 billion dollars by doing random activities
such as unboxing toys, reviewing different games, and playing with
fictional characters. At the age of 12, he has one of the biggest
YouTube channels on the platform.
```

**When to use:** Success stories, self-made celebrities, unlikely rises

---

### Type 4: The Tragic Backstory Hook

**Pattern:** Struggles list + "wasn't always" + Current success + "Untold story"

```
Homelessness, traumatic childhood, and losing both parents at a young
age, Joey Diaz wasn't always the comedian we all have come to love. He
fought many battles, including a severe drug addiction and trouble with
the law. However, he never let go of his dreams, and today, he is one of
the most renowned stand-up comedians.

This is the untold story of Joey Diaz.
```

**When to use:** Redemption stories, comeback narratives, respected figures

---

### Type 5: The "What If You Found Out" Exposé Hook

**Pattern:** Build public image + "But what if..." + Dark truth

```
Millions of dollars in cash, insane game prizes, extraordinary
challenges, and a spending habit that one can't even fathom. All of
these are things that made Mr. Beast one of the biggest YouTubers out
there today. But what if you found out that everything that goes on in
these videos is just a scam? In fact, it is not even close to reality
and everything is scripted to gain more views and generate revenue.
```

**When to use:** Scandal exposés, revealing content, "the truth about" videos

---

### Type 6: The Rivalry Hook

**Pattern:** Both names + Rivalry context + Jealousy/conflict angle

```
Okay, so, it's the Angel Reese and Caitlyn Clark rivalry once again.
Ever since Clark became one of the most famous WNBA players, Angel
Reese's jealousy has increased significantly, and Angel Reese isn't even
hiding it anymore.
```

**When to use:** Beef videos, comparison content, rivalry documentaries

## CELEBRITY DOCUMENTARY STRUCTURE (12-20 min)

### HOOK (0:00-1:00)
Use one of the hook types above. Keep it under 100 words.

---

### EARLY LIFE / ORIGIN (1:00-4:00)
Where they came from.

```
[B-ROLL: Hometown footage, childhood photos if available]

[CELEBRITY] was born on [DATE] in [LOCATION].

[Family background - brief, relevant details only]

[Early signs of who they'd become OR contrast to current image]

[Key formative experience or turning point]

But [CELEBRITY]'s life was about to change dramatically...
```

**What to include:**
- Birth date, location (establishes timeline)
- Family situation (only if relevant to story)
- Early struggles or advantages
- The moment that set them on their path

---

### THE RISE (4:00-8:00)
How they got famous.

```
[B-ROLL: Early career footage, first viral moments]

[CELEBRITY]'s big break came when [specific event].

[CLIP: Early interview or performance]

[How they capitalized on initial success]

[Key decisions that accelerated their rise]

[Collaborations, viral moments, turning points]

By [YEAR], [CELEBRITY] had [achievement milestone].
But success brought its own challenges...
```

**Structure the rise as:**
1. The break (how they got noticed)
2. The climb (key moves they made)
3. The peak (height of success/fame)

**Connect each phase with BUT/THEREFORE:**
- "They went viral, BUT that attracted haters, THEREFORE they had to..."
- "Success came fast, BUT so did the pressure, THEREFORE..."

---

### THE CONTROVERSY / PEAK (8:00-14:00)
The drama everyone came to see.

**For scandal content:**
```
[B-ROLL: News coverage, social media screenshots]

Then came the moment that changed everything.

On [DATE], [the incident].

[CLIP: The viral moment or reaction]

The internet exploded.

[Public reaction - tweets, news coverage]

[Celebrity's response - or lack thereof]

But [CELEBRITY]'s defenders/critics had something to say...

[Both sides of the controversy]

[Additional incidents that compounded the situation]
```

**For non-scandal content (career peak):**
```
At the height of their fame, [CELEBRITY] was [achievements].

[Specific numbers - followers, earnings, awards]

[What made them different from others]

[The moment that defined their peak]

But even at the top, cracks were starting to show...
```

---

### THE AFTERMATH / CURRENT (14:00-18:00)
Where things stand now.

```
[B-ROLL: Recent footage, current projects]

Today, [CELEBRITY] [current status].

[How they've addressed the controversy - or haven't]

[What they're doing now]

[Public perception - has it changed?]

[Recent developments]

[What the future might hold]
```

**For redemption arcs:**
```
It took [TIME], but [CELEBRITY] managed to [redemption moment].

[What they learned]

[How they've changed]

[Current success/status]
```

**For ongoing controversies:**
```
As of [DATE], [CELEBRITY] still faces [ongoing issues].

[Current legal status, public perception]

[What experts/insiders say]

```

### CTA / REFLECTION (18:00-20:00)
Wrap it up with perspective.

```
[CELEBRITY]'s story shows us that [broader lesson].

Whether you love them or hate them, [acknowledgment of complexity].

[Optional: Tease of related content]

If you want to see how [SIMILAR CELEBRITY] dealt with a similar
situation, that video is on screen now.

Subscribe for more stories like this.
```

## CONTROVERSY STACKING TECHNIQUE

Don't reveal all controversies at once. Stack them:

**Layer 1 (Hook):** The headline controversy
**Layer 2 (Minutes 4-6):** "But this wasn't the first time..."
**Layer 3 (Minutes 8-10):** "And then things got even worse..."
**Layer 4 (Minutes 12-14):** "But the biggest revelation was yet to come..."

Each layer should be connected by a rehook that promises more drama.

## REHOOKS FOR CELEBRITY CONTENT

**After revealing controversy:**
- "But this wasn't even the tip of the iceberg..."
- "And the allegations didn't stop there..."
- "What happened next made things even worse..."

**Before major revelations:**
- "But before we get to that, you need to understand..."
- "This is where things get really interesting..."
- "However, the real story was just beginning..."

**For ongoing drama:**
- "As of this recording, [CELEBRITY] still hasn't addressed..."
- "The situation is still developing..."
- "We'll have to wait and see how this plays out..."

## CELEBRITY DOCUMENTARY TYPES

### The Exposé
Focus: Revealing hidden truth
Tone: Investigative, slightly critical
Structure: Public image → Cracks → Evidence → Revelation

### The Biography
Focus: Complete life story
Tone: Neutral to admiring
Structure: Origin → Rise → Peak → Legacy

### The Downfall
Focus: How they fell from grace
Tone: Cautionary, dramatic
Structure: Peak → First mistake → Spiral → Current state

### The Comeback
Focus: Redemption arc
Tone: Inspirational
Structure: Success → Fall → Rock bottom → Recovery → New success

### The Rivalry
Focus: Two celebrities in conflict
Tone: Dramatic, balanced
Structure: Both origins → Intersection → Conflict → Escalation → Current state

---

## BALANCING ACT

Celebrity docs require balance:

1. **Be fair** - Show multiple perspectives
2. **Verify claims** - Stick to confirmed facts
3. **Humanize** - They're people, not just characters
4. **Don't defame** - Allegations should be labeled as such
5. **Acknowledge complexity** - Avoid black-and-white narratives
