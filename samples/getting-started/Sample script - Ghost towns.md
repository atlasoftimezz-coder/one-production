# Ghost Towns (sample)

**Note:** This is a short sample to try One Production. Lines like this one, and the titles, are not read aloud.

## The town that stopped

Pripyat was built for the workers of a power plant, and for sixteen years it was one of the most comfortable towns in the country. Then, in the spring of 1986, it emptied in a single afternoon. People were told they would be back in three days. Nobody came back.

## What was left behind

Today the school still has books on the desks, and the fairground wheel has never turned. Trees grow through the floors of apartment blocks. It is not a ruin from a distant past. It is a town that simply stopped, with everything still in its place.
